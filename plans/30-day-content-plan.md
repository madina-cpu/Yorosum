# Yorosum Collagen — 30-Day Content Plan

**Channels:** Instagram (main) + Telegram channel (same content plus prices and order links) · **Language of posts:** Russian (Korean versions for KR Smart Store/KakaoTalk where needed) · **This plan is written in English** and is meant to be translated or adapted into Russian captions.

## Ground rules

1. **No product ads in Week 1.** First make the page look alive and human.
2. **Pace:** 1 feed post a day at most, with 3–5 stories a day. Never dump many posts at once.
3. **Content mix for the month:** 40% brand story and lifestyle, 30% education (ingredients, how to use), 20% social proof (reviews, bloggers, B2B partners), 10% direct offers.
4. **Claims:** say what the product contains and how you use it. **Never** say it treats, cures, removes wrinkles, stops ageing or fixes joints. Avoid before/after photos. Check local rules in UZ and KR.
5. **Shoot in batches:** shoot a week's photos and videos in one session (good daylight, plain background, phone is fine).
6. **Every post ends with one simple CTA:** follow, save, comment, or "order via Telegram bot / Uzum" (the last one only from Week 2).

**Formats:** 📷 photo post · 🎠 carousel · 🎬 Reel (15–30 s) · ⭕ stories

## Story routine (every day, about 10 minutes)

- Morning: product in your routine (1 story)
- Day: behind the scenes, orders, deliveries (1–2 stories)
- Evening: poll / question / repost of customers (1–2 stories)
- **Story highlights to create:** About · Ingredients · How to use · Reviews · Where to buy · B2B

## Telegram channel adaptation

- Repost every feed post (same text, plus a direct link to the bot / Uzum).
- Extra Telegram-only posts, 2x a week: price list, delivery info, "in stock" updates, short promos.
- Pin one message: what it is, price, how to order, delivery cities.

Avoid medical hashtags (anything mentioning a disease or treatment).

## Weekly batching workflow with AI (about 2 hours a week)

1. **Sunday, 30 min:** give AI next week's 7 rows from this plan plus the product facts sheet, and ask for Russian captions (short, friendly, no medical claims) with 3 hook variations each.
2. **Sunday, 60 min:** shoot all photos and Reels for the week in one session.
3. **Sunday, 20 min:** schedule Instagram/Facebook in Meta Business Suite (free) and Telegram posts with built-in scheduling.
4. **Daily, 10 min:** stories plus replying to comments and DMs (keep this human, don't automate replies at the start).
5. **End of week, 10 min:** note the top 2 posts by saves/shares and make more like them next week.
