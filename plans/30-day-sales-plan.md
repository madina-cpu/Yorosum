# Yorosum Collagen — 30-Day Sales Plan

**Owner:** solo founder (no staff) · **Markets:** Uzbekistan (UZ) + South Korea (KR) · **Stock:** 1,500 units in each country

## 1. Goal for the first 30 days

Get the first sales, get the first reviews and sign the first repeat B2B buyers, on a small budget. Wholesale (B2B) brings in volume and cash. Direct sales (B2C) build the brand and bring in reviews.

### Targets (starting assumptions; adjust after Week 2)

| Market | Channel | Units | Price | Revenue |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| UZ | B2B (clinics, salons, gyms, pharmacies) | 300 | 120,000 sum | 36,000,000 sum |
| UZ | B2C (Telegram, Instagram, Uzum) | 100 | 250,000 sum (promo floor: decide, e.g. 199,000) | ~22–25M sum |
| KR | B2B (shops, gyms, skin clinics, CIS community stores) | 150 | 10,500 won + VAT | 1,575,000 won + VAT |
| KR | B2C (Naver Smart Store, Coupang, Russian-speaking community) | 100 | 34,900 list / 19,900 promo | ~2.5–3M won |
| **Total** | | **650 units (~22% of stock)** | | |

Free samples are on top of this: **max 60 units** (30 UZ, 30 KR). Every sample must lead to either a blogger post or a B2B decision.

## 2. Pricing rules (decide once, stick to them)

- **UZ B2C 250,000 sum is the reference price.** Promo price only during announced campaigns (e.g. "launch week −20%").
- **UZ B2B 120,000 sum.** Minimum order: 10 units. For 50+ units, offer a free display or free samples instead of a price cut, so resellers keep their margin.
- **KR B2C:** 34,900 list. **19,900 only during short campaigns** (max 3–5 days). Normal discount range: 24,900–29,900.
- **KR B2B:** 10,500 + VAT, minimum 20 units.
- **Never undercut your B2B buyers** in your own B2C channels. If they see you selling cheaper than they can, they stop reordering.

## 3. Before Day 1: confirm these (blockers)

- [ ] Expiry date of both batches (it decides how hard you push B2B)
- [ ] UZ: product registration / sanitary certificate in hand (pharmacies and Uzum will ask for it)
- [ ] KR: business registration + 통신판매업 신고 (mail-order business report, needed for Naver/Coupang); food category confirmed (일반식품 vs 건강기능식품), because it decides which claims are allowed
- [ ] Product facts sheet: collagen type and source, mg per serving, format, flavour, servings per box, other ingredients, OEM origin ("Made in Korea")

## 5. B2B outreach script (base text, translate to RU/UZ/KO)

> Hello! I'm [name], founder of Yorosum — a collagen supplement made in Korea. I think it fits your clients well. Wholesale is 120,000 sum, recommended retail 250,000 sum, so your margin is over 50%. Can I bring you a free sample this week so you can try it yourself?

**Follow-up (3 days later):**
> Just checking in, did you get a chance to look? I can start you with just 10 units, and if they don't sell, we'll talk about an exchange.

(Only offer an exchange/return if you can afford it, and decide the terms in advance.)

## 7. Day-30 review questions

1. Which channel sold the most units per hour of my time?
2. Which B2B type (clinic, salon, gym, pharmacy, community store) reordered?
3. What were the top 3 objections, and how do I answer them?
4. At this pace, when will each 1,500-unit stock run out, and is that before the expiry date?
5. What do I stop, keep, and double down on in Month 2?

## 8. AI shortcuts for a solo founder

- **Lead lists:** ask AI to draft search queries and categories, then check each lead yourself on Google/Yandex Maps.
- **Outreach:** generate RU/UZ/KO versions of the scripts and personalise the first line per lead.
- **Listings:** generate titles, bullets and keywords for Uzum / Naver / Coupang from the facts sheet.
- **Weekly review:** paste the sheet numbers into Claude and ask for "stop / keep / double down" recommendations.
- **Compliance check:** run every caption and listing through a claims check before posting (no "treats/cures/removes wrinkles").
