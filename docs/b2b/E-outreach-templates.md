## 6. 15-minute discovery call script

1. **Intro (1 min):** who you are, why you're calling them specifically.
2. **Ask before you pitch (6 min):**
   - Which channels do you sell through? (pharmacy, marketplaces, beauty retail)
   - Which collagen / beauty supplements do you carry now? What sells and at what price?
   - What do you need from a new supplier? (margin, exclusivity, marketing, registration)
   - How do you usually handle registration (СГР / UZ)? Who holds the certificate?
   - What would a typical first order look like?
3. **Pitch, tailored (4 min):** link 2–3 benefits to what they just told you.
4. **Next step (2 min):** samples + document pack → proposal → second call date.
5. **After the call:** recap email the same day with the agreed next steps; update the CRM.
