# Regulatory & Claims Checklist

> This is a compliance screen, not legal advice. Rules and fees change, so items marked **(verify)** must be confirmed with the authority or a licensed local registration agent before you rely on them.

---

## 1. Claims: one rule for all four markets

**State what is IN the product. Never state what it DOES to the body.**

| ✅ Safe (factual composition) | ❌ Risky / prohibited |
|---|---|
| "Low-molecular fish collagen peptide 1,000 mg per stick" | "Reduces wrinkles", "improves skin elasticity" |
| "Contains vitamin C [x] mg" | "Anti-aging", "inner beauty", "edible cosmetics" |
| "Made in a GMP/HACCP-certified facility in Korea" | "Boosts immunity", "treats joint pain" |
| "Average molecular weight [x] Da (per supplier spec)" | "Absorption rate 90%" (unless you hold the study and it is permitted) |
| "Halal certified by [body]" (if held) | Before/after photos, testimonials about effects |

Keep the **evidence file** for every factual claim (COA, ingredient spec, certificates). In Korea, MFDS can demand substantiation within **15 days**.

## 2. 🇰🇷 South Korea — 식품표시광고법

### Step 1: Classification decides what you can say
| Class | Pre-review | What you may say |
|---|---|---|
| **일반식품** (most collagen products) | None required | Composition only. **No functional claims at all.** All 제8조 prohibitions apply. |
| **기능성표시식품** | KFIA pre-review **mandatory** (incl. detail page), ~₩165,000/case, ~20 working days | Only the functional ingredient's recognised function, in approved wording |
| **건강기능식품** (e.g., 개별인정형 저분자콜라겐펩타이드) | KHFF pre-review | Only the function recognised for **that specific raw material**, in approved wording |

⚠️ **The most common, most expensive mistake:** a 건강기능식품 유통전문판매업 licence does **not** let you make HFF claims on a 일반식품. And an ingredient supplier's 개별인정 recognition is **not** inherited by your product unless the product itself is registered as 건강기능식품 using it.

### Step 2: Screen every Korean asset for the 10 prohibitions (제8조제1항)
1. 질병 예방·치료 효능 · 2. 의약품 오인 · 3. **건강기능식품 오인** · 4. 거짓·과장 · 5. 소비자 기만 (testimonials, before/after, unsupported mechanism) · 6. 타사 비방 · 7. 부당 비교 · 8. 사행심·음란 · 9. 비식품 오인 · 10. 미심의 광고

**Flag words for collagen:** 영양제, 면역력, 보습, 탄력, 주름, 안티에이징, 흡수율, 이너뷰티, 먹는 화장품, 슈퍼푸드, 다이어트, 피부 개선, any disease name.

**Also check:** product name, keyword/tag fields (Naver/Coupang search tags count as advertising), image text, and the **layout**: an ingredient amount placed next to a glowing-skin photo is judged 소비자 기만.

### Penalties
- 제8조 ① 1–3호 → up to 10 years or ₩100M · 4–10호 → up to 5 years or ₩50M
- Administrative: business suspension up to 6 months
- **Liability falls on you (유통전문판매업자/brand owner), not the OEM.**

### Licences you need (verify)
- If you sell an OEM-made product under your own brand: **식품(건강기능식품) 유통전문판매업** registration with your local 시·군·구 office, matching the product class.
- Mail-order business report (통신판매업 신고) for online sales.

## 3. 🇰🇿 Kazakhstan & 🇰🇬 Kyrgyzstan — EAEU rules

| Item | Requirement (verify current details) |
|---|---|
| Product category | Usually **БАД (биологически активная добавка к пище)** |
| **State registration (СГР / Свидетельство о государственной регистрации)** | Mandatory for БАД. Issued by an authorised body in **one** EAEU state and valid across the EAEU (KZ, KG, RU, BY, AM). Recorded in the EAEU unified register. One registration covers both KZ and KG. |
| Technical regulations | TR CU 021/2011 (food safety), TR CU 022/2011 (labelling), TR CU 029/2012 (additives), plus the EAEU Uniform Sanitary Requirements for БАД |
| Dossier typically includes | Composition and specification, manufacturer's documents (GMP/HACCP/ISO 22000), lab test protocols from an accredited EAEU lab, label layout, instructions for use, origin documents |
| Applicant / registration holder | Often the importer. **Try to hold the СГР yourself or through a company you control**, so a distributor change doesn't trap your product |
| Marking | **EAC** mark after conformity |
| Labelling | **Kazakhstan:** Kazakh + Russian. **Kyrgyzstan:** Russian, plus Kyrgyz **(verify)**. Must include "БАД. Не является лекарственным средством", composition, daily dose, storage, shelf life, manufacturer and importer |
| Claims | No disease or treatment claims. Wording must match the СГР and the approved instructions |
| Timeline / cost | Commonly ~1–3 months; cost varies by agent and testing **(verify by quote from 2–3 agents)** |

## 4. 🇺🇿 Uzbekistan (not in EAEU)

| Item | Requirement (verify; procedures were reformed in recent years) |
|---|---|
| Registration | **Separate national state registration** for БАД through the Ministry of Health / sanitary-epidemiological authority **(verify current body)** |
| Conformity | Conformity certification via the national standards and certification system **(verify)** |
| Dossier | Similar to EAEU: specs, manufacturer certificates, lab tests, label |
| Labelling | **Uzbek (Latin script) mandatory**, Russian commonly added |
| Halal | Not mandatory but commercially strong; the Uzbek halal authority's certificate is valued |
| Practical tip | Use a Tashkent registration agent; ask the distributor to recommend one, but **you** keep copies and, ideally, ownership of the registration |

## 5. Document pack to prepare once (reused in every market)

- [ ] Product specification (composition per serving, raw material origin, molecular weight)
- [ ] Certificate of Analysis (COA) per batch
- [ ] OEM factory certificates: GMP (건기식 GMP if applicable), HACCP, ISO 22000/FSSC 22000
- [ ] Raw material certificates (collagen supplier spec, origin, halal if any)
- [ ] Certificate of Free Sale / 수출 위생증명서 (export health certificate) **(verify which documents each market requests)**
- [ ] Certificate of Origin (Korea Chamber of Commerce; Korea–Uzbekistan/Kazakhstan trade terms may apply)
- [ ] Halal certificate (if available)
- [ ] Label artwork (KO, RU/KZ, RU/KY, UZ/RU)
- [ ] Trademark registration certificates
- [ ] Shelf-life / stability data
- [ ] Translations (notarised if required)

---

## 6. Pre-publish checklist (use for every asset, in every language)

- [ ] Product classification for this market noted
- [ ] All claims are composition or fact only, no body effects (unless permitted for this class and pre-reviewed)
- [ ] No disease names, no "medicine" wording
- [ ] No testimonials, before/after shots or effect-implying imagery next to ingredient amounts
- [ ] Keyword/tag fields and product name checked (not just body copy)
- [ ] Evidence file exists for every factual number
- [ ] Not copied from OEM or ingredient-supplier marketing material
- [ ] Wording matches the registration (СГР / UZ registration) for CA markets
