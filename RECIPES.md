# YOROSUM Glow Peptide — 20 Recipes

## Refreshing Drinks & Teas

### 1. Sparkling Peach Collagen Ade
- **Ingredients:** 1 stick Yorosum Glow Peptide, 200 ml plain sparkling water or club soda, 1 tsp honey, ice, fresh mint leaves.
- **How to make:** Stir 1 stick of Yorosum with the honey into 2 tbsp of warm water until dissolved. Pour over a glass of ice, top with sparkling water, and garnish with mint.

### 2. Iced Peach Green Tea "Glow"
- **Ingredients:** 1 stick Yorosum Glow Peptide, 250 ml brewed green tea (chilled), ice, lemon slice.
- **How to make:** Dissolve 1 stick into the cooled green tea, add ice, and stir well. Finish with a squeeze of fresh lemon for a bright, citrusy note.

### 3. Peach Hibiscus Iced Tea
- **Ingredients:** 1 stick Yorosum Glow Peptide, 200 ml tart hibiscus tea (brewed and chilled), ice cubes.
- **How to make:** Whisk the powder stick directly into the chilled hibiscus tea for a tangy, ruby-peach drink.

### 4. Peach Cream Earl Grey Latte
- **Ingredients:** 1 stick Yorosum Glow Peptide, 150 ml chilled Earl Grey tea, 50 ml oat milk or almond milk, ice.
- **How to make:** Mix the Yorosum powder into the cold Earl Grey tea. Pour over ice and top with cold milk for a peachy milk tea.

### 5. Peach Electrolyte Mocktail
- **Ingredients:** 1 stick Yorosum Glow Peptide, 200 ml coconut water, a pinch of sea salt, lime juice.
- **How to make:** Shake the Yorosum powder, coconut water, lime juice, and sea salt with ice in a shaker. Perfect for post-workout refreshment.

## Smoothies & Protein Shakes

### 6. Peach Vanilla Protein Smoothie
- **Ingredients:** 1 stick Yorosum Glow Peptide, 1 scoop vanilla protein powder, ½ cup frozen peach slices, 200 ml almond milk.
- **How to make:** Blend everything on high speed until thick and creamy.

### 7. Tropical Peach Mango Smoothie
- **Ingredients:** 1 stick Yorosum Glow Peptide, ½ cup frozen mango chunks, ½ frozen banana, 150 ml coconut water.
- **How to make:** Blend the fruit, coconut water, and Yorosum stick until smooth.

### 8. Green Glow Peach Smoothie
- **Ingredients:** 1 stick Yorosum Glow Peptide, 1 cup fresh spinach, ½ green apple, ½ cup frozen peaches, 200 ml water.
- **How to make:** Blend the spinach and water first, then add the remaining ingredients and the Yorosum stick and blend again.

### 9. Berry Peach Hydrator Shake
- **Ingredients:** 1 stick Yorosum Glow Peptide, ½ cup frozen mixed berries, ½ cup Greek yogurt, 150 ml water or milk.
- **How to make:** Blend until smooth to combine the sweet berries with the peach flavor.

### 10. Peach Coconut Cream Smoothie
- **Ingredients:** 1 stick Yorosum Glow Peptide, 50 ml canned coconut milk, 150 ml almond milk, ½ frozen banana, ice.
- **How to make:** Blend everything together for a creamy, tropical, dessert-style smoothie.

## Yogurt, Bowls & Breakfasts

### 11. Peach Collagen Greek Yogurt Bowl
- **Ingredients:** 1 stick Yorosum Glow Peptide, 150 g plain Greek yogurt, fresh fruit (sliced peaches/berries), granola, honey drizzle.
- **How to make:** Stir the Yorosum powder into the Greek yogurt until smooth, then top with fresh fruit, granola, and a drizzle of honey.

### 12. Peach & Chia Seed Pudding
- **Ingredients:** 1 stick Yorosum Glow Peptide, 2 tbsp chia seeds, 150 ml milk of choice, ½ tsp vanilla extract.
- **How to make:** Whisk the Yorosum stick, chia seeds, vanilla, and milk together. Cover and refrigerate overnight.

### 13. Peach & Honey Overnight Oats
- **Ingredients:** 1 stick Yorosum Glow Peptide, ½ cup rolled oats, ½ cup almond milk, 1 tbsp chia seeds, 1 tsp honey.
- **How to make:** Combine the oats, chia seeds, milk, honey, and Yorosum stick in a jar. Stir well and refrigerate overnight.

### 14. Peach Smoothie Bowl Base
- **Ingredients:** 1 stick Yorosum Glow Peptide, 1 cup frozen peaches, ¼ cup thick Greek yogurt or ½ frozen banana.
- **How to make:** Blend with minimal liquid until thick like soft-serve. Scoop into a bowl and top with chia seeds and sliced almonds.

### 15. Peach Cottage Cheese Power Bowl
- **Ingredients:** 1 stick Yorosum Glow Peptide, ½ cup low-fat cottage cheese, sliced fresh peaches, a handful of walnuts.
- **How to make:** Fold the Yorosum powder into the cottage cheese, then top with fresh fruit and nuts for a high-protein breakfast.

## Frozen Treats & Desserts

### 16. Peach Collagen Ice Pops
- **Ingredients:** 2 sticks Yorosum Glow Peptide, 300 ml coconut water or peach juice, fresh peach slices.
- **How to make:** Dissolve the Yorosum powder into the coconut water. Place peach slices in popsicle molds, pour the liquid over them, and freeze for 4–6 hours.

### 17. Peach Frozen Yogurt Bites
- **Ingredients:** 1 stick Yorosum Glow Peptide, 1 cup Greek yogurt, 1 tbsp honey, silicone bite molds.
- **How to make:** Mix the Yorosum powder, yogurt, and honey. Spoon into silicone molds and freeze for 2 hours.

### 18. Peach Jelly Cubes
- **Ingredients:** 2 sticks Yorosum Glow Peptide, 200 ml hot water, 1 tbsp gelatin powder (or 1 tsp agar-agar powder), 1 tbsp honey.
- **How to make:** Dissolve the gelatin and honey in the hot water (if using agar-agar, bring it to a boil for 1–2 minutes so it sets). Let it cool slightly, stir in the 2 sticks of Yorosum, pour into a square dish, and chill in the fridge until firm. Cut into cubes.

### 19. Peach Collagen Sorbet
- **Ingredients:** 1 stick Yorosum Glow Peptide, 1½ cups frozen peach slices, 2 tbsp lemon juice, 2 tbsp warm water.
- **How to make:** Dissolve the Yorosum stick in the warm water. Blend the frozen peaches, lemon juice, and Yorosum liquid in a food processor until smooth and slushy.

### 20. Peach Green Tea Popsicles
- **Ingredients:** 2 sticks Yorosum Glow Peptide, 300 ml brewed sweet green tea, lemon slices.
- **How to make:** Mix the Yorosum powder into the cooled green tea, pour into popsicle molds with a slice of lemon, and freeze overnight.
