## 5. Answers to common questions

**"You don't own a factory. Is that a risk?"**
No. The factory is a specialist that produces at scale for many brands under strict Korean food-safety supervision (MFDS). We hold the formula and brand rights, and our manufacturing agreement guarantees supply continuity and quality standards.

**"Can you guarantee the same quality every batch?"**
Yes. Each batch is tested and comes with its own COA, and we keep retained samples for the full shelf life.

**"What happens if you change factories?"**
The formula and specification belong to [BRAND]. Any change is managed under the same specification, and we notify registered partners in advance as registration rules require.

**"Can we visit or audit?"**
We can arrange documentation reviews and, by agreement with the manufacturer, a factory visit or video audit.
