## 1. The core strategy in one paragraph

You are one person with one SKU, so your job is **not** to run four markets yourself. Your job is to **sign one strong partner per market and supply them well.** Distributors, importers and marketplace operators handle the in-country work (warehousing, pharmacy listings, sales reps, local ads). You handle the product, the documents, pricing, partner support and brand content. Every activity in this plan is chosen to fit that model.

**Recommended sequence (assumes the product is made in Korea; see §2):**

| Phase | Market | Role | Why this order |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 (now) | 🇰🇷 Korea | **Proof base** | Domestic sales, reviews and listings become your "sold in Korea" credentials for Central Asia. It is also the most heavily regulated market, so start with the low-cost online channels. |
| 1 (months 1–6) | 🇰🇿 Kazakhstan | **Primary export market** | Largest and richest of the three Central Asian markets, strong K-beauty demand, and Kaspi.kz plus pharmacy chains give you reach. An EAEU registration obtained here also covers Kyrgyzstan. |
| 2 (months 4–9) | 🇰🇬 Kyrgyzstan | **Low-cost add-on** | Same EAEU registration and Russian-language materials. Often served by the KZ distributor or a small Bishkek importer. |
| 3 (months 6–12) | 🇺🇿 Uzbekistan | **Growth market** | Large population (~37M) and fast-growing e-commerce (Uzum Market). It is outside the EAEU, though, so it needs a **separate registration** and an Uzbek-language label. |

## 2. Facts to pin down first (they change the plan)

> **Answered from your product documents (see `03-product-fact-sheet.md`):**
> - **Factory:** Korea CNS Pharm, Eumseong, Korea, so **"Manufactured in Korea"** is confirmed. Collagen raw material is Indian freshwater fish (ATHOS), so say "Made in Korea", not "Korean collagen".
> - **Classification:** 기타가공품 (general food). **Composition-only claims in Korea.** The carton's "이너뷰티 루틴" line is flagged; see `04-label-compliance-review.md`.
> - **Source:** freshwater fish (tilapia, catla, mrigal), with no pork/bovine collagen. A strong point for UZ/KZ/KG. Halal certificate still to be investigated.
> - **Format:** 3 g powder stick × 30, peach, 24-month shelf life. Light and easy to ship.
> - **Batch:** first run 2,700 boxes (RA88201, best before 2028-09-15).
> - **Still open:** unit cost, re-order MOQ/lead time, master carton data, HACCP/GMP certificates, trademark filings abroad.


| Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Where is the OEM factory?** (Korea or elsewhere) | "Made in Korea" is a major selling point in Central Asia. If the product isn't Korean-made, Korea becomes an *import* market (MFDS import declaration) and your Central Asia positioning changes. |
| **Korean classification:** 일반식품 / 기능성표시식품 / 건강기능식품? | This decides **what you're legally allowed to say in Korea** (see `02-regulatory-checklist.md`). Most collagen products are 일반식품 and **cannot** claim skin, wrinkle or moisture effects. |
| **Collagen source and type** (fish/porcine/bovine; peptide/tripeptide; Dalton size) | Fish collagen is easier to sell in majority-Muslim UZ/KZ/KG. Porcine collagen is a serious barrier there. |
| **Halal certificate?** | A strong door-opener in UZ, KZ and KG. Ask your OEM; many Korean OEMs can arrange one. |
| **Format and dose** (powder stick, liquid shot, tablet, jelly) | Affects shipping cost, shelf life and channel fit (liquid shots are heavy to ship, sticks travel well). |
| **OEM MOQ, lead time, shelf life, unit cost** | Drives your pricing, minimum order terms and cash planning. |
| **Do you own the brand/trademark?** | Register the trademark in KZ/UZ/KG **before** you share materials with distributors. Distributors registering a supplier's brand themselves is a known risk in the region. |

## 3. Market snapshots

### 🇰🇷 South Korea — proof base
- **Situation:** One of the most saturated collagen markets in the world. Hundreds of brands compete, many with celebrity endorsements. Competing head-on in mass retail isn't realistic for a solo founder.
- **Where a solo founder can win:**
  - **Naver Smart Store + Coupang** (own listings): cheap to start, and they build your review base.
  - **Closed malls (폐쇄몰) and welfare malls (복지몰)** for employee benefits: B2B-like, accept small brands, low marketing spend.
  - **Specialist B2B wholesalers / 공구 (group-buy) influencers**: commission-based, no upfront fee.
  - **Clinics (피부과·성형외과) and pharmacies**: a niche, but credible.
  - **Export agents and trading companies** who already ship K-food to Central Asia. They're a second route into the other three markets.
- **Biggest risk:** advertising law (식품표시광고법 제8조). Every Korean asset must pass the compliance screen in `02-regulatory-checklist.md`. Liability sits with **you as brand owner**, not the OEM.

### 🇰🇿 Kazakhstan — primary export market
- **Demand drivers:** strong affinity for K-beauty and K-culture, a growing middle class in Almaty/Astana, and pharmacies as the trusted place to buy supplements (БАД).
- **Channels:**
  - **Pharmacy chains** (e.g., Europharma, Sadyhan, Biosfera, **(verify current major chains)**). The highest-trust channel. Usually reached through a pharma distributor.
  - **Kaspi.kz**: the dominant marketplace and payment platform. A local partner can list for you.
  - **Wildberries / Ozon (KZ)**: an extra channel. Needs a local or EAEU legal entity, or a partner.
  - **K-beauty shops and cosmetics chains**: collagen fits next to skincare.
  - **Instagram / Telegram / TikTok** influencers: the main consumer discovery channels.
- **Language:** B2B in Russian. Labels in **Kazakh + Russian**.

### 🇰🇬 Kyrgyzstan — add-on market
- Smaller market (~7M people). Price-sensitive, with Bishkek as the hub.
- Channels: pharmacy chains (e.g., Neman **(verify)**), K-beauty stores, Instagram shops, Lalafo, Wildberries.
- **Simplest route:** ask your KZ distributor to cover KG, or appoint a small Bishkek importer under the same EAEU registration.
- Labels in **Kyrgyz + Russian (verify current requirement)**.

### 🇺🇿 Uzbekistan — growth market
- Large, young population. Fast e-commerce growth (**Uzum Market** is the leader). Korea is popular (large Korean diaspora, K-dramas).
- Not an EAEU member, so it needs **its own state registration** and conformity documents **(verify current procedure with a local agent)**.
- Channels: pharmacy chains in Tashkent, Uzum Market, Instagram/Telegram shops (Telegram is huge in UZ), beauty salons and clinics.
- Labels in **Uzbek (Latin script) + Russian (verify)**. B2B in Russian or Uzbek.

### Message pillars — B2B vs. consumer

| Pillar | B2B message (distributor) | Consumer message (CA markets*) |
|---|---|---|
| Origin | "Made in Korea by a certified manufacturer: an easy story for your sales team" | "Korean beauty standard" |

## 5. B2B sales process (solo-friendly)

### 5.1 Build the target list (20–40 per market)
Sources:
- Pharmacy distributor and chain websites; LinkedIn ("дистрибьютор БАД", "импортер косметики Корея").
- **KOTRA** trade offices in Almaty and Tashkent (KOTRA runs buyer-matching, often free or subsidised for Korean SMEs).
- Trade fairs: **KIHE** (Almaty, healthcare), **TIHE** (Tashkent, healthcare), **Central Asia Beauty Expo**, and in Korea **Health Functional Food Expo** and **K-Beauty Expo**, where CA buyers visit **(verify current dates)**.
- **Korean SME export programmes** (중진공, KOTRA 수출바우처, 지자체 해외마케팅 지원). These can pay for trade-fair booths, translation and registration costs. **Check them before you spend your own money.**
- Wildberries / Kaspi / Uzum: sellers already listing Korean supplements are warm prospects.

Track everything in one simple CRM sheet: `Company | Country | Type | Contact | Channel | Stage | Next step | Date`.

### 5.2 Pipeline stages
1. **Researched** → 2. **First contact** (email + LinkedIn/WhatsApp/Telegram) → 3. **Replied / call booked** → 4. **Samples + docs sent** → 5. **Terms negotiated** → 6. **Trial order** → 7. **Repeat order / exclusivity discussion**

### 5.3 What to send at each stage
| Stage | Documents (in `docs/b2b/`) |
|---|---|
| First contact | Outreach email (`E-outreach-templates.md`) + **Product One-Pager** (`A-product-one-pager.md`) |
| Call booked | **OEM & Quality Brief** (`B-oem-quality-brief.md`) |
| Serious interest | **Partnership Proposal** with price list and terms (`C-partnership-proposal.md`) + samples + document pack (COA, GMP/HACCP certificate, ingredient spec, label artwork) |
| Contract | Distribution agreement (have a lawyer review; key terms in Proposal §6) |

### 5.4 Communication norms
- **Central Asia:** relationships come first. Expect WhatsApp/Telegram, video calls and, ideally, one visit. Russian is the B2B language. A Russian-speaking founder is a big advantage; otherwise use a professional translator for documents (not machine-only).
- **Korea:** email + KakaoTalk, formal documents, quick replies expected.

## 6. Pricing framework

Price backwards from the **shelf price**, not forwards from cost.

```
Retail shelf price (target, local currency)
 − VAT (KR 10% · KZ 12%→16% from 2026 (verify) · KG 12% · UZ 12% (verify))
 − Retailer margin            (pharmacy ~25–40%, marketplace commission ~10–25%)
 − Distributor margin         (~20–35%)
 − Import duty, registration amortisation, logistics, customs broker
 = Your FOB/EXW price  → must be ≥ OEM cost × 1.6–2.0 to stay healthy
```

**Steps:**
1. Benchmark 5–10 competing collagen products on Kaspi / Uzum / Wildberries / pharmacy sites in each market.
2. Pick a target shelf price (premium Korean: usually above local brands, below Western premium brands).
3. Work backwards. If your EXW price comes out too low, change the format or pack size, or pick a different channel.
4. Offer **tiered EXW prices** (trial order, standard, volume), not discounts on request.

Use the price table in `C-partnership-proposal.md`.

## 7. Marketing support you can realistically provide (solo)

Distributors will ask what marketing support you give. Offer a **fixed, cheap-to-produce kit** instead of cash:

1. **Product photo pack** (white background, lifestyle, infographic): one shoot, reused everywhere.
2. **Marketplace content pack**: title, bullet points and description in RU / KZ / UZ / KO, all compliance-checked.
3. **Short video pack** (5–10 vertical clips, 15–30 s) for Reels/TikTok, with subtitles in local languages.
4. **Pharmacist / sales-rep training sheet**: one page of FAQs and how to explain the product.
5. **Launch co-marketing**: free samples for influencers (e.g., 50–100 units per market) instead of a cash budget.
6. **Marketing development fund (optional):** 2–5% of order value as free goods or a credit, tied to proof of activity.

## 11. Main risks & mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Regulatory delays (СГР / UZ registration take months) | Start the dossier in month 2; use an experienced agent; sign distributor contracts *conditional* on registration |
| Distributor registers your trademark or holds the registration certificate hostage | File your trademark first. Make sure **you** (or a company you control) are the registration certificate holder or applicant where possible, or state ownership in the contract |
| Illegal claims (fines are personal liability in KR) | Every asset passes the compliance checklist; never reuse OEM or ingredient-supplier marketing copy |
| Cash tied up in stock | Small trial orders; prepayment (30–50%) or LC; OEM MOQ negotiation |
| Single-SKU dependence | Use partner feedback to choose SKU #2 by month 9 (offer private-label OEM to distributors as a second revenue line) |
| Founder burnout | One market at a time; outsource translation, registration and logistics |
