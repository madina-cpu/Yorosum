## 5. Answers to common questions

**"You don't own a factory. Is that a risk?"**
No. The factory is a specialist that produces at scale for many brands under strict Korean food-safety supervision (MFDS). We own the brand, the product is registered under our name as distributor (유통전문판매원), and every batch is released against a fixed specification with a COA. *[If you have a written supply/quality agreement with CNS Pharm, say so here; if not, consider signing one before you sign distributors.]*

**"Can you guarantee the same quality every batch?"**
Yes. Each batch is tested and comes with its own COA, and we keep retained samples for the full shelf life.

**"What happens if you change factories?"**
The formula and specification belong to YOROSUM / Silla Global Trading. Any change is managed under the same specification, and we notify registered partners in advance as registration rules require.

**"Can we visit or audit?"**
We can arrange documentation reviews and, by agreement with the manufacturer, a factory visit or video audit.
