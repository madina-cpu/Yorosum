Page 2 · What is collagen?

Collagen is the spring inside your skin

  • 1

    Collagen is a protein. It makes long, strong threads inside your skin, bones and joints.

  • 2

    Think of a mattress. Collagen threads are the springs that keep skin firm and bouncy.

  • 3

    As we get older, the body slowly makes less collagen. That is why people buy collagen drinks and sticks.

There are different types. For skin, you want Type I.

Type I

Skin, hair, nails, bones. About 9 out of 10 parts of your body's collagen. Comes from fish or cow.

Type II

Joints and knees. Comes from chicken. Not used for skin products.

Type III

Skin and blood vessels. Works next to Type I. Comes from cow or pig.

All 9 products here use fish collagen. Fish collagen is Type I, the skin type. Good.
Page 3 · What is "Da"?

Da tells you how big the collagen pieces are. Smaller is better.

  • 1

    Da (dalton) is how scientists weigh very small things like molecules. Big number = big piece. Small number = small piece.

  • 2

    Collagen is a long chain of tiny beads. Makers cut the chain into short pieces so the body can take it in.

  • 3

    The gut takes in pieces of 2 or 3 beads most easily. That is about 300 Da. Pieces of 1,000 or 2,000 Da must be cut smaller by the body first.

Look for "평균분자량" (average molecular weight) on the box. 300 Da or lower is the best you can buy.

Page 5 · Vitamin C

Vitamin C is the builder that turns collagen pieces into skin

  • 1

    Collagen pieces are like bricks. Vitamin C is the builder. Without it, the body cannot build strong new collagen.

  • 2

    Vitamin C does not push collagen into the blood. Its job comes after that: it helps build the collagen in your skin.

  • 3

    A good stick gives about 100 mg, one full day of vitamin C. We give 10/10 at 100 mg or more.

Page 6 · Tiny extras

Don't be fooled by fancy words on the box

Many boxes say "Glutathione", "Biotin" or "Hyaluronic acid" in big letters. But the amount inside is often a tiny drop. A tiny drop does nothing.

A long list of extras is not a sign of quality. Check the amount in mg. If it is tiny, ignore it.
Page 7 · Reading the box

Find the real collagen amount in 3 steps

Many boxes show a percentage (%), not milligrams. Here is how to turn it into mg. Example: HURUM Small Molecular.

1

Find the collagen %.

60%
2

Find the weight of one stick (포 = stick). 1 g = 1,000 mg.

2 g = 2,000 mg
3

Multiply. This is the real collagen in one stick.

2,000 × 60% = 1,200 mg

Watch out for big box numbers

The SSF box says "100,000 mg" collagen. That is for the whole box of 100 sticks. One stick has 1,000 mg. Always count per stick.

Page 8 · Fair price

Compare price per gram of collagen, not price per box

Boxes have 30 to 100 sticks, so the box price can trick you. Divide the price by the total grams of collagen in the box. Shorter bar = cheaper collagen.

BOTO has the cheapest box (₩8,630). But its collagen costs more per gram than HURUM or Yorosum, and the pieces are big (2,000 Da).
Page 9 · Your checklist

6 questions before you buy

  • Is it fish collagen (Type I)?
  • Is the Da on the box, and is it 300 Da or lower?
  • Is there at least 1,000 mg collagen per stick? 2,000 mg or more is best.
  • Is there about 100 mg vitamin C per stick?
  • Are the extras big enough to matter, or just tiny drops?
  • What is the price per gram of collagen?
